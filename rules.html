Rules, terms and privacy

Draft for review by the club advisor and school. Last updated November 2026. Questions: hello@thestudyhub.us

Terms of use

The Study Hub is a free student club. High school volunteers who speak English and another language help elementary students with homework over Google Meet. By using this site you agree to these terms.

Who can use it

  • Only a parent or legal guardian can request a session for a child. Children do not create accounts.
  • Tutors must be current students at the school, approved by a club officer, and must agree to the Tutor Code of Conduct.

What we offer

  • Help is free. We never charge families or ask for payment information.
  • Tutors are student volunteers, not licensed teachers. We help children understand homework. We do not do the homework for them, and we can't promise grades or results.
  • Tutors may decline a request, for example if the homework is outside what they can help with or the time no longer works.

Using the site respectfully

  • Share only homework. Do not upload photos of people, ID cards or anything unrelated.
  • Be kind and respectful in every message and session.
  • The club may pause or remove any family or tutor who breaks these rules.

Limits

The site and sessions are offered as is. The Study Hub and its volunteers are not responsible for what happens outside a session, for technical problems with Google Meet, or for homework results. We may change these terms. If we make an important change, we will tell families by email.

Privacy policy

We collect as little as we need, and we only collect a child's information from their parent or guardian, with their consent.

What we collect

  • From parents: your name and email.
  • About your child: first name, grade, language, subject and the homework photos you choose to upload.
  • From tutors: name, grade, school email, languages, subjects and their Google Meet link.

How we use it

  • To match your child with a tutor and send you the session link.
  • We never sell information, show ads or share it with anyone outside the club.

Who can see it

  • Your child's request and photos are visible only to the tutor you picked, club officers and the club advisor.

How long we keep it

  • Homework photos are deleted 30 days after the session.
  • Requests are deleted at the end of the school year.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, fix or delete your family's information at any time by emailing hello@thestudyhub.us. We will reply within 10 school days.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time. We will then delete your child's information and cancel any sessions.

Children's privacy

Because the students we help are under 13, we follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Only a parent can submit a child's information, and the parent consents on the request form. We do not collect last names, addresses, school names or photos of children.

Service providers

To run the site we use Supabase (database, sign-in and private photo storage), GitHub Pages (website hosting), Resend (sending emails) and Google Meet (video calls). They process information only to provide these services.

Parent or guardian consent and release

By checking the consent boxes on the request form, I confirm that:

  • I am the child's parent or legal guardian.
  • I give permission for my child to join a video tutoring session on Google Meet with a high school volunteer from The Study Hub.
  • I consent to The Study Hub collecting my child's first name, grade and homework photos as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • An adult will be nearby during every session, and I may end a session at any time.
  • I understand tutors are student volunteers, not licensed teachers, and that help is free and offered as is.
  • I release The Study Hub, its volunteers and the school from claims related to taking part, except where the law doesn't allow it.
  • I can withdraw this consent at any time by emailing hello@thestudyhub.us.

Tutor code of conduct

  • Use only your school Google account and the Meet link in your profile on thestudyhub.us.
  • Keep every conversation about homework. Talk to children only during scheduled sessions, never by text, social media, phone or private chat.
  • Never ask for or share personal information such as last names, addresses, phone numbers or social media.
  • Do not record sessions or take screenshots of a child.
  • Keep your camera on, sit somewhere appropriate and dress as you would for school.
  • Help the child learn. Explain steps instead of giving answers.
  • If a parent isn't nearby, ask the child to get them. If an adult can't be found, end the session politely.
  • If something worries you, or a child tells you about harm or danger, tell the club advisor that day.
  • Approve or decline requests within 48 hours, and cancel at least 24 hours ahead if you can't make it.
  • Breaking these rules can mean removal from the club.

Session safety rules

  • Sessions last about 30 minutes, on weekdays between 3:30 and 6:30 pm.
  • Only the tutor, the child and their family join the call.
  • A parent or guardian stays nearby for the whole session.
  • Sessions are not recorded.
  • Either side can end a session at any time. Report any problem to hello@thestudyhub.us.
