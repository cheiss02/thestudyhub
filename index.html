How it works

  1. Pick a timeChoose a tutor who speaks your family's language and tap an open yellow time.
  2. Send the homeworkAdd photos of the worksheet so the tutor can prepare. No account needed until you send.
  3. Get the Meet linkWhen the tutor approves, we email you the Google Meet link, plus a reminder 1 day and 1 hour before.

Questions parents ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Every session is free. Tutors are student volunteers and we never ask for payment information.

Who are the tutors?

High school students who speak English and at least one other language. A club officer approves every tutor, and tutors follow our code of conduct.

What does my child need?

A phone, tablet or computer with a camera, Google Meet, and the homework. A parent or guardian books the session and stays nearby during the call.

Which languages do tutors speak?

Spanish, Mandarin, Korean, Vietnamese, Arabic, Tagalog and more, depending on which volunteers are available each week.

What happens to the homework photos?

Only the tutor you chose and club officers can see them, and they're deleted automatically 30 days after the session. Read our privacy policy.
