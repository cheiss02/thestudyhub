MEET THE FOUNDER

Hi, I'm Liz!This is why I started The Study Hub.

Liz Flores · Junior at Cerritos High School · Founder

I grew up in a house where homework time could get stressful. Not because my parents didn't care. They cared so much. The problem was that the homework was in English, and English wasn't their first language.

They wanted to help. They just couldn't always read the directions with me. So a lot of nights I figured it out on my own, and I know I'm not the only one.

Right now there's a little kid somewhere staring at a worksheet they can't read yet, with a parent next to them who wants to help but doesn't have the words. That's who The Study Hub is for.

Many of our parents left their land looking for a better future for us. Now it's our turn to give them a hand.

Liz Flores
