Parents

Book free homework help

For elementary students who are learning English. Tutors help with the hard part in 30 minutes or less. They don't do the whole homework.

  1. Go to the siteOpen thestudyhub.us on a phone, tablet or computer.
    • Type thestudyhub.us in your browser (Chrome, Safari or Edge all work).
    • At the top right, tap Get started. On a phone it may be inside the Menu button (three lines).
    • Choose I'm a parent or guardian. Kids don't make their own accounts: the parent books for them.
    • Prefer Spanish? Tap Español at the bottom of any page for a guide in Spanish.

    Tip: You can look at tutors and open times before signing up. You only need an account to send a request.

  2. Sign in with your emailNo password to remember. We send you a code instead.
    • Type your email and tap Continue. Use an email you check often: that's where Meet links and reminders go.
    • Open the email from The Study Hub (no-reply@thestudyhub.us). It has a 6-digit code and a sign-in button.
    • Same device? Tap the button in the email. Different device (email on your phone, site on your computer)? Type the 6 digits in the box on the website.
    • Next time, tap Sign in. On the same device the site remembers you and shows Continue as your@email.

    Tip: No email after 2 minutes? Check spam or "Promotions", then tap Send a new email. Always use the newest code.

  3. Tell us about your familyA one-time profile so a club admin can approve you.
    • Fill in your name, your city, the language you speak at home and how you heard about us.
    • Check the box to agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy, then tap Send for approval.
    • A club admin reviews every new family to keep kids safe. This usually takes 1–2 days.
    • You'll get an email when you're approved. Until then the site shows "waiting for approval" and you can't book yet.

    Tip: We only ask for your child's first name and grade, never their last name or school.

  4. Find a tutor and a timeChoose a tutor who speaks your language, then a yellow time.
    • Tap Find a tutor at the top, or scroll down the home page to the tutor cards.
    • Use the language buttons (Spanish, Mandarin…) to see only tutors who speak your family's language.
    • Use ‹ › to change weeks. You can book up to 8 weeks ahead.
    • Yellow times are open. Striped "Booked" times are taken. Tap a yellow time to start a request.

    Tip: A tutor shows "No open times this week"? Tap See next week → on their card.

  5. Send the homeworkTell the tutor what your child is stuck on and add photos.
    • Your name fills in for you. Add your child's first name, grade, language and subject.
    • In Which part is your child stuck on?, be specific: "doesn't understand the directions for the word problems on page 2."
    • Tap Choose files and add 1 to 6 photos of the homework. Cover your child's last name and school first.
    • Check all the boxes (you're the parent, an adult stays nearby, the tutor helps with the hard part for 30 minutes or less, and you agree to the terms), then tap Send request.

    Tip: You can have up to 3 requests waiting at a time. Wait for an answer before sending more.

  6. Get your Meet linkThe tutor approves or declines, and we email you.
    • Approved: you get an email with the Join Google Meet button and any note from the tutor.
    • Declined: the tutor can't make it. Pick another time or tutor. It's still free.
    • We send reminders 1 day and 1 hour before the session.
    • Everything is also in My requests (top menu), including the Meet button.

    Tip: Can't make it? Open My requests and tap Cancel session so the tutor knows.

  7. Join the sessionBe ready a few minutes early.
    • Use a device with a camera and microphone. Install the Google Meet app on a phone or tablet if needed.
    • Have the homework, a pencil and the worksheet ready.
    • Tap Join Google Meet at the session time. An adult must stay nearby for the whole call.
    • Sessions are 30 minutes or less. The tutor explains the hard part, then your child finishes the rest.

    Tip: Sessions are never recorded. Either side can end the call at any time.

  8. See your historyTrack your child's progress.
    • After the session the tutor logs it. In My requests, Session history shows what your child worked on.
    • You'll see the total time for each child and which tutors helped.
    • Homework photos are deleted automatically 30 days after each session. You can delete everything anytime at the bottom of My requests.
Tutors

Volunteer as a tutor

For high school students who speak English and another language. Every session you log counts toward your volunteer hours.

  1. Sign upCreate your account with your school email.
    • Go to thestudyhub.us → Get started → I'm a high school student.
    • Use your school email. Reminders and new requests go there.
    • Type the 6-digit code from the email, or tap the button in the email if you're on the same device.

    Tip: Code not arriving? School email can be slow or filtered. Check spam, then tap Send a new email.

  2. ApplyTell us about yourself. Families see your name, grade, languages, subjects and intro.
    • Name: first name and last initial only (for example, Sofia R.).
    • Languages: check every language you can tutor in besides English. Add any other language in the box.
    • Subjects: what you can help with (Reading, Writing, Math, Science, Spelling).
    • Intro: one or two friendly sentences for families. Why you want to tutor is only seen by club officers.
    • Check the box for the Tutor Code of Conduct and tap Send application.
  3. Get approvedA club officer reviews you before families can see you.
    • You'll see "Thanks! A club officer will review your application" on the Tutor dashboard.
    • Sign the code of conduct with the club advisor.
    • When you're approved you get an email, and Tutor dashboard opens with four tabs: Requests, My schedule, History, Profile.
  4. Add your Google Meet linkFamilies get this link when you approve them.
    • Open meet.google.com signed in with your school Google account (not a personal Gmail).
    • Tap New meeting → Create a meeting for later and copy the link (it looks like meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij).
    • In Tutor dashboard → Profile, paste it in Your Google Meet link and tap Save profile.
    • Use the same link for every session. You can't approve requests until it's saved.
  5. Set ① Every weekYour usual times. Set them once.
    • Go to My schedule. The first part is ① Every week.
    • Tap a box to open that time (it turns yellow). Tap it again to close it.
    • These times repeat every week automatically. Families see them on Find a tutor.
    • Closing a time doesn't cancel sessions that are already booked.

    Tip: Start with 2–3 times a week you can really keep. You can add more later.

  6. Use ② Calendar for one dateChanges for one specific day only.
    • The second part is ② Calendar. Use ‹ › to pick the week (up to 8 weeks ahead).
    • Tap a yellow Open time to turn it Off for that date only (a game, a test, a family event).
    • Tap an empty box to add an Extra time (blue) for that date only.
    • Tap Day off under a date to close the whole day. Tap Undo to bring it back.
    • Booked sessions show the child's name in green and can't be changed here. To cancel one, use Requests.

    Tip: Use ① for your normal week and ② only for exceptions. You don't need to fill in every week by hand.

  7. Answer requestsYou decide which sessions to take.
    • New requests appear in Requests with a number on the tab, and you get an email.
    • Open a request to see the child's first name, grade, subject, the family's note and the homework photos (tap a photo to make it big).
    • Tap Approve if you can help. The family gets your Meet link by email. Add an optional note, like "please have the reading book ready."
    • Tap Decline if you can't make it or the homework isn't something you can help with. Answer within 48 hours.
    • You get reminders 1 day and 1 hour before each session.
  8. Tutor and mark it doneCoach, don't solve, then log it.
    • Open your Meet link a few minutes early. Make sure a parent is nearby.
    • Explain the hard part (directions, a new word, the first problem), then let the child try. Keep it to 30 minutes or less.
    • After the start time, the card says How did it go? Tap Mark as done.
    • Choose whether the child joined, how long it lasted, and write what you worked on (the family can see this note).
    • Your total volunteer time shows at the top of the dashboard and in History, with every child you helped.

    Tip: Need proof of volunteer hours? Show your History page, or ask a club officer.

Club officers

Run the club

Only for people on the admin list.

  1. Sign inOnly emails on the admin list can open Club admin.
    • Go to thestudyhub.us/admin.html and tap Admin sign-in.
    • Use your admin email and the 6-digit code. A Club admin link then appears in the menu.
  2. Approve peopleNobody can book or tutor until you approve them.
    • New families: check the name, city and language, then Approve family or Decline. They get an email either way.
    • Tutor applications: approve only after they sign the code of conduct with the advisor.
  3. Keep it runningDay-to-day tools.
    • Tutors table: pause a tutor who can't tutor for a while (their times hide from families). Volunteer time per tutor is listed here.
    • Sessions table: see every session and its status. Cancel if needed (both sides get an email).
    • Impact: help hours, sessions completed, requests and new families.
  4. Manage admins and start freshAdmins and resets.
    • Club admins: add an email to make someone an admin, or remove them. The main help@ account can't be removed.
    • Reset on any Impact counter starts it from zero today. Records stay.
    • After testing: remove test tutors and families, then Delete all sessions and photos (type DELETE to confirm).
